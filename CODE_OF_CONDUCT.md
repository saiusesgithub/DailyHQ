# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

DailyHQ contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Welcome people regardless of experience or background, discuss ideas rather than individuals, and accept feedback in good faith.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's security reporting channel. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior is harmful to the community.

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
